About Shivank Kirana Store

Meerut ka bharosemand grocery & FMCG store — last updated July 2026

📖 Our Story

Shivank Kirana Store ki shuruaat ek chote se sapne se hui thi — Meerut ke logon ko fresh, quality grocery products unke ghar ki pragal par pahunchana. Humari journey 2024 mein shuru hui, jab Shivank So Om Pal ne dekha ki logon ko rozana ki shopping ke liye ghar se bahar jaana padta tha.

Aaj, hum Meerut ke premium grocery delivery platform ke roop mein jaane jaate hain, jahan aapko Amul, Haldiram's, Britannia, Cadbury, Lay's jaise 20+ top brands ke 500+ products milte hain — seedha aapke darwaze tak.

🎯 Hamara Goal: "Har ghar tak quality grocery — ek tap mein!"

🎯 Our Mission & Vision

Mission: Meerut ke har ghar mein affordable, fresh aur genuine grocery products pahunchana, bina kisi jhanjhat ke.

Vision: Uttar Pradesh ka sabse trusted online kirana platform banana, jahan customers quality, speed aur convenience — teeno ek saath paayein.
