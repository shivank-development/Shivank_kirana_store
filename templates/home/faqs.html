💳 Payment

Hum UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) aur Cash on Delivery (COD) accept karte hain. COD orders ke liye ₹49 advance fee (UPI se) zaroori hai.
Agar payment fail ho toh phir se try karein ya COD option chunein. Agar amount kaat gaya lekin order confirm nahi hua, toh 24 hours mein automatically refund ho jayega.
₹49 COD advance isliye lagta hai taaki delivery confirm ho. Yeh amount final bill mein adjust ho jata hai — sirf ek confirmation fee hai.
