Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 July 2026 — Last updated: 14 July 2026

📋 Data We Collect

Jab aap Shivank Kirana Store use karte hain, hum kuch zaroori information collect karte hain:

  • Personal Info: Naam, email address, phone number
  • Delivery Info: Delivery address, pincode, GPS location (agar aap allow karein)
  • Order Info: Aapke past orders, cart items, wishlist
  • Payment Info: UPI transaction ID ya COD advance confirmation (hum card details store nahi karte)
  • Device Info: Browser type, IP address, device model
🔐 Hum aapka password kabhi plain text mein store nahi karte — sirf encrypted form mein.

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

Collected data ka use sirf in kamon ke liye hota hai:

  • Orders process karna aur delivery arrange karna
  • Customer support provide karna
  • Order status notifications bhejne ke liye (SMS/WhatsApp)
  • Website experience improve karna
  • Fraud prevention aur security
⚠️ Hum aapka data kisi third-party marketing company ko nahi bechte.

🔗 Data Sharing

Hum kuch limited cases mein data share karte hain:

  • Delivery Partners: Aapka naam aur address delivery boy ko order deliver karne ke liye
  • Payment Gateways: UPI transaction verify karne ke liye
  • Legal Requirements: Agar court ya government order aaye

🍪 Cookies Policy

Hamari website cookies use karti hai jo aapke browser mein store hoti hain. Cookies help karti hain:

  • Login session maintain karne mein
  • Cart ka data save karne mein
  • Website traffic analyze karne mein

Aap apne browser settings mein ja kar cookies disable kar sakte hain, lekin kuch features kaam nahi karenge.

🛡️ Your Rights

  • Access: Aap apna stored data dekh sakte hain
  • Delete: Account delete karne par saara data hata diya jayega
  • Update: Aap kabhi bhi apni profile update kar sakte hain
  • Opt-out: Marketing messages se unsubscribe karne ka haq
