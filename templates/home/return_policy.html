Return & Refund Policy

Hassle-free returns — aapki satisfaction hamari priority hai

📅 Return Window

⚡

Same-Day Returns

Damaged/Wrong item — delivery ke time pe hi report karein

📅

7-Day Returns

Quality issue ya wrong product

❌

No Return

Perishable items (bread, dairy) — delivery ke baad

✅ Return Eligible Items

  • Sealed/unopened packaged products (FMCG, snacks, beverages)
  • Wrong product received (e.g., 500g ki jagah 250g deliver hua)
  • Damaged packaging — product delivery ke time damaged tha
  • Expired product — expiry date pehle se cross ho chuki thi
  • Missing items from order
✅ Return eligible items ki original packaging honi chahiye.

🚫 Non-Returnable Items

  • Khule hue ya use kiye gaye products
  • Fresh dairy products (milk, curd) — ek baar open hone ke baad
  • Perishable items (fresh bread, vegetables)
  • Cosmetics/personal care — agar sealed packaging khol di gayi ho
⚠️ Agar aapko delivery ke time koi issue dikhe, turant delivery boy ko batayein ya WhatsApp karein.

📋 Return Process (Step-by-Step)

  1. Delivery ke time ya baad mein issue notice karein
  2. WhatsApp par +91 7599342112 pe message karein
  3. Order number aur product photo bhejein
  4. Hamari team 2 hours ke andar respond karegi
  5. Return pickup arrange ki jayegi (ya store drop-off)
  6. Product inspect hone ke baad refund ya replacement process hoga

💰 Refund Process

  • UPI Payments: 2-3 business days mein original UPI ID par refund
  • COD Orders: UPI ya bank transfer ke zariye refund
  • Store Credit: Aap chahein toh wallet mein credit bhi le sakte hain (instant)
💡 Store credit sabse fast option hai — turant available!

📦 Damaged Items Policy

Agar delivery ke time product damaged mile:

  • Delivery boy ke saamne hi package check karein
  • Damaged item ki photo lein
  • Delivery accept karne se pehle humse contact karein
  • WhatsApp pe photo bhejein — hum replacement ya full refund denge
